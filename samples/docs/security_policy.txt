Enterprise Security Policy - AI SOC Copilot Sample Document

1. Authentication Requirements
All users must enable MFA. Failed login attempts exceeding 5 within 15 minutes trigger account lockout.

2. IAM Security
- Principle of least privilege must be enforced
- AssumeRole calls require approval for cross-account access
- CreateUser and AttachUserPolicy events require security review

3. Incident Response
- Critical incidents: respond within 1 hour
- High severity: respond within 4 hours
- All incidents require post-mortem documentation

4. Log Retention
CloudTrail, firewall, and Kubernetes audit logs retained for 365 days.

5. Threat Intelligence
Privilege escalation, failed authentication bursts, and suspicious IAM activity are priority alerts.
